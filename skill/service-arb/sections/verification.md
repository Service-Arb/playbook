# verification

## what matters
- the course's own videos are the baseline for what to show: signage, the vehicle, a business card. a member's failure story qualifies them, it does not replace them // inferred
- one phone can submit videos for several profiles
  [r2 2026-09-18](https://www.loom.com/share/c197556c940b4d01b53376dab70d32e5?t=1603)

## how to decide
- video: a pre-recorded video with no edits passed. keep the edits few and generate no part of it fully with AI; whether cutting two clips together passes is untested
  [r3 2026-09-18](https://www.loom.com/share/c197556c940b4d01b53376dab70d32e5?t=429)
- live: only after a rejected video, through a support ticket. the course lesson and the ticket walkthrough it links are the procedure
  [r1 2026-08-29](https://www.skool.com/gmp-passive-profits-5347/classroom/ca84165f?md=44258ff1e40e4c68b18fb8857d44e39f)
- live: open several tickets at once, so a failure has a spare
  [r2 2026-09-18](https://www.loom.com/share/c197556c940b4d01b53376dab70d32e5?t=3473)
- asked to show cars: say the employees are out on jobs
  [r3 2026-09-11](https://www.loom.com/share/1eae977e2bc84cbd8b80a3b38506fe9f?t=200)
- anything that injects or edits video has a shelf life of weeks. check the newest capture before relying on it // inferred

## when it goes wrong
- a failed video is not the end of the listing. the next attempt is live verification, not a second identical video // inferred
- a strict reviewer on one live call is a data point about that reviewer. for location, the course says to hang up and call again hours later for another agent
  [r1 2026-08-29](https://www.skool.com/gmp-passive-profits-5347/classroom/ca84165f?md=44258ff1e40e4c68b18fb8857d44e39f)
