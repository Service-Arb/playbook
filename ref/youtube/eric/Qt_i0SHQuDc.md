## summary

- Find contractors through Nextdoor, Facebook groups, Google My Business, text-message blasts, or Indeed; the speaker says a blast can produce many applicants within an hour and proposes a 60/40 commission split.
- Start with a trial phase of two to three jobs before formal onboarding, checking communication, punctuality, service quality, customer satisfaction, and honesty by letting the contractor collect payment and confirming the amount with the customer.
- Hold a 30–40-minute Google Meet and use an onboarding sheet to explain procedures, expectations, payment processing through a CRM, Square, or Stripe, quoting, customer updates, receipts, materials, job photos, and upselling.
- Have the contractor handle fieldwork while you manage marketing, calls, scheduling, follow-ups, and customer service; require timely communication, no customer stealing, on-time payment, and agreed payment handling.
- Maintain the relationship by paying contractors on time, communicating well, discussing what increases revenue, and tracking close rate and average ticket, with the goal of delegating operations and scaling toward multiple daily jobs and tens of thousands in monthly revenue.
