## summary

- Rank a service-area Google Business Profile by outcompeting nearby listings on reviews, keywords, and profile optimization rather than relying only on an address.
- Target high-ticket niches and smaller cities or suburbs where the top three listings often have under 10 reviews; examples include Nashville concrete contractors and Myrtle Beach plumbers.
- Use SEMrush for keyword research, prioritizing service-and-city names such as “Plumber Myrtle Beach”; the example keyword research showed “plumber” outperforming “plumbing” and about 10,000 monthly searches in the market.
- Build roughly 10–40 five-star reviews, complete the hours, photos, and updates, and exploit competitors’ limited opening times—especially evenings and Sundays—to capture leads.
- Confirm proof of concept by finding service-area businesses already ranking, then audit competitors’ reviews, keywords, photos, hours, updates, and overall SEO to identify one to three areas where you can beat them.
