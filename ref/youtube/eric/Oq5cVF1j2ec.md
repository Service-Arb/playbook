## summary

- Builds a recurring local-service customer base through Google My Business (GMB) profiles, then sells the contracts for roughly 10–12 times monthly revenue; Joseph reportedly grew pool cleaning to $9,000/month and sold it for about $100,000 in 55 days.
- Chooses recurring services such as pool cleaning, pest control, lawn care, or cleaning, then uses Semrush to target cities with about 1,000+ monthly searches and Google Maps to assess top-three competitors, reviews, ratings, proximity, and keyword-matched business names.
- Creates and optimizes a GMB profile with a service-and-city keyword name, address, category, phone, domain, hours, AI-written description, services, photos, and reviews; the speaker recommends matching or exceeding the lowest top-three review count.
- Converts inbound calls into recurring contracts, charges customers monthly or weekly, hires fulfillment contractors through Indeed, Facebook Marketplace, or Nextdoor, and organizes customers into a service route.
- Once the route has recurring revenue and operating history, lists it with a local service-business broker, sells the customer contracts while retaining the GMB profile for future leads, and repeats by creating additional profiles or keeping the cash flow.
