## summary

- Builds revenue by creating and ranking Google Business Profiles (GBPs) for local services, generating calls and leads without paid ads.
- Monetization options include contracting jobs on a 60/40 split, selling calls for about $80, renting profiles for roughly $2,000 monthly, and charging at least $1,000 for verified listings or $1,000–$2,000 for reinstatements.
- The process is to select low-competition, high-demand cities, use Google Maps and Semrush for review and search-volume research, verify the profile, optimize its name and sections with ChatGPT-generated keyword content, and obtain local reviews.
- Examples include 5,000 monthly searches producing roughly five leads per day, a plumbing profile earning up to $10,000 monthly, $30,000 profit in the second month, and a student reporting $40,000 revenue and $29,000 profit.
