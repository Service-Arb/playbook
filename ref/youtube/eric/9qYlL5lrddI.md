## summary

- Diagnose a call decline by checking whether the Google Business Profile dropped in local ranking; a move from second to fourth can reduce roughly 30 monthly leads to far fewer.
- Maintain activity with one to two genuine reviews and one to two updates or photos per week; daily posts, offers, events, and geotagged photos are unnecessary.
- Separate sustained declines from normal fluctuations caused by seasonality, weather, and daily search volume, and evaluate calls and interactions month to month.
- Improve conversion with a clean website, instant booking when appropriate, trustworthy real-world cover photos, a prominent phone number and booking call-to-action, a 4.8-plus rating, current hours, and regular profile updates.
- For high-volume profiles, monitor competition and rankings in Google Maps, prioritize areas with high search volume and low competition, and keep listings consistently updated.
