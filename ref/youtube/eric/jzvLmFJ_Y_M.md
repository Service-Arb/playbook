## summary

- The model lists local services on Google, ranks them in the map pack, charges customers, and pays contractors roughly 50% of revenue to fulfill jobs.
- It recommends low- to mid-ticket niches such as house cleaning, car detailing, plumbing, HVAC, and electrical, then selects cities using competition, reviews, keywords, and Semrush search volume.
- The setup uses Google Business Profile, CallRail ($50/month with five numbers, then $3 each), domains, ChatGPT, and a simple website from Lovable or Hostinger.
- The revenue math targets about $100 profit per detailing job, three jobs daily for $10,000/month, and roughly 30 reviews per profile to compete; contractors are sourced through Facebook Marketplace, groups, Nextdoor, and cold outreach.
- Fulfillment requires fast phone answering, booking appointments immediately, using guarantees, hiring contractors on commission or fixed payouts, and eventually adding a Philippine virtual assistant for about $5/hour.
