## summary

- Scale auto-detailing service arbitrage beyond the $10K–$15K/month bottleneck by automating high call volume, which can require about 15 leads and four to five bookings daily at a 33% conversion rate.
- Hire Philippine virtual assistants through OnlineJobs.ph, screen them with Loom or voice recordings, and have them answer calls, book appointments, update Google Sheets or a CRM, coordinate contractors, handle customers, and manage follow-ups.
- Increase revenue per customer by raising a $250 service toward $300, adding paint corrections at $500+, ceramic coatings at $800+, and upsells such as pet hair removal, headlight restoration, engine-bay cleaning, second cars, referrals, reviews, and maintenance plans; raising average order value from $250 to $350 can add roughly $10,000 monthly at three jobs per day.
