## summary

- Structure remote service arbitrage around recurring monthly, weekly, or biweekly plans instead of relying only on new leads; recurring-friendly examples include pool cleaning, house cleaning, pest control, lawn care, and HVAC maintenance.
- Prioritize communication, punctuality, politeness, follow-up, and customer care: the speaker says roughly 90% of the service experience comes from the person and contractors often lose customers by answering poorly or being unreliable.
- After a successful first job, offer an incentive such as a maintenance plan discount; the example changes a $250 car detail into $150 monthly visits, producing $1,800 over a year versus $500 from two one-off jobs.
- Sell convenience by scheduling visits automatically, offer monthly and biweekly options, repair complaints with refunds or return visits, and use a CRM for scheduled texts and customer tracking once revenue reaches a few thousand dollars per month.
- The speaker reports making about $10,000 in one week by renewing past detailing customers onto recurring plans and describes a listing model where he partners with local contractors and takes 40% of job revenue.
