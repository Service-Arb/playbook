# **5️⃣ How to Upsell**

Here is the script that has worked very well for us. 

---

## **⭐ 1\) The Inspection Script (Use this on EVERY call)**

When you arrive:

`Hey just so you know, as part of our inspection fee we also offer to take a look at your whole plumbing system to make sure we don’t miss anything. So obviously we're gonna address the main issue first but after I'm done I can do a quick walk through to see if everything else looks good, sometimes there's small issues that if you catch early are a lot easier to take care of before they turn serious.` 

`When you do the actual work - talk to the customer, be human, and try to establish a good relationship with the customer so they like you.`

`Then once you're done, explain to them what was wrong and why this happened. Tell them that whatever you worked on was slightly concerning (blame the last plumber for doing it wrong, or say something got ruined over the years, make up a reason on why this needs extra attention) and offer another more permanent solution for their main concern. Ex - snake into a camera inspection, show them everything wrong in the pipes from the camera, offer hydrojet`

`It will look something like this:`

**`So I got the main issue taken care of. Here’s what was happening and what caused it. This issue happens fairly often, and usually when I see this, there are a couple ways to prevent it from happening again.”`**

`Then present 2–3 realistic options:`

### **`Option 1 — Basic fix (low ticket)`**

`(Already done, the minimum work)`

### **`Option 2 — Better fix (medium ticket)`**

`(something that stops the issue from reoccurring)`

### **`Option 3 — Best / long-term solution (very high ticket in the thousands)`**

`(repairs + replacement + upgrades)`

`Customers almost always choose the middle option.`

`This is EXACTLY what big plumbing companies do.`

`This makes it more likely for them to go with the middle option because it doesn't seem super extreme but still almost just as good as the third very expensive option.` 

`.` 

**Since this problem came up, it’s usually a good idea to double-check the other common plumbing points — sometimes they’re connected. I can look around quickly while I’m here, no charge.”**

This hits three psychological triggers:

✔ They see you as a professional  
 ✔ They trust you more  
 ✔ They EXPECT to hear more findings

This approach is used by the highest-performing service companies in North America.

Not shady, not pushy — *smart*.

# **🔥 MOST IMPORTANT PART: WHAT TO LOOK FOR \+ WHAT TO UPSELL (Legitimate, High-Margin Items)**

Here’s EXACTLY what your plumbers should upsell — all ethical, common, and valuable:

---

# **⭐ TOP REALISTIC UPSSELLS (the ones that ALWAYS convert)**

## **🧰 1\) Drain calls (your biggest ticket size potential)**

Upsell path:

1. **Snake**  
    → $200–$350

2. **Camera inspection**  
    → $150–$250

3. **Hydrojet**  
    → $350–$900

4. **Replace bad section / cleanout install**  
    → $250–$1,200+

Why this works:  
 Camera makes the problem VISUAL → easy close.

---

## **🚽 2\) Toilet calls**

Upsell path:

* New fill valve

* New flapper

* Complete rebuild

* New toilet install

* Wax ring \+ flange repair

* Shutoff valve replacement

Tickets easily go from $120 → $400–$700.

---

## **🔧 3\) Leaks**

Upsell path:

* Replace angle stops

* Replace supply lines

* Replace trap

* Rebuild section of pipe

* Recaulk sinks/tubs

* Replace faucet / upgrade fixture

---

## **🚿 4\) Showers & tubs**

Upsell path:

* Cartridge replacement

* New trim kit

* Flow check

* Regrout/reseal

* Pressure balancing valve work

---

## **🛁 5\) General home inspection upsells**

During the walk-through, look for:

* Corroded shutoffs

* Old copper pipes

* Missing cleanouts

* Slow drains

* Backflow preventer requirements

* Loose or rocking toilets

* Aged faucets

* Water pressure issues

* Old water heater (easy $1,500–$3,000 ticket)

* S-traps, illegal traps

Every single thing here is a REAL find.  
 You’re not inventing problems — you’re identifying neglected ones.

And the customer will often thank you for catching issues early.

### **For water heater calls:**

* Sediment flush ($149–$199)

* New heater quote

* Expansion tank

* New shutoff valve

**The goal per job:**

### **➜ Minimum $500**

### **➜ Aim for $700–$1500 with bundles**
