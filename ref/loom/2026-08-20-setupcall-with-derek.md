### How to record verification videos 0:00

- Start the video at a local street sign or relevant exterior location even if recording from home.
- Videos can all be recorded from home and reused across profiles by showing different signage.
- Sequence recommendation: show street sign, company signage (on car), business card, company dashboard on a device, then start the vehicle and show tools.

### Visual elements to include 0:20

- Include company signage on a vehicle and a business card (printed paper is acceptable).
- Show tools and equipment to demonstrate business activity; nothing specialized required for verification.
- Good-looking signage and a clear business card improve credibility but are not strictly necessary.

### Mailing address vs. recording location 1:21

- Use the home address as the mailing address if recording at home.
- Still record local street signs and show tools to support the listing.
- Ensure the service area includes the geographic area the profile should appear in (e.g., include Queens if Flushing is used).

### Service area selection guidance 2:00

- Choose service areas that cover target listing locations (example: Queens, Brooklyn, Manhattan).
- If targeting multiple boroughs, include each borough and cities in between to broaden coverage.
- Confirm Flushing is within Queens when selecting service area for that neighborhood.

### Address addition and approval strategy 3:14

- Adding an address is a volume game; many submissions will be rejected.
- Improve approval chances by waiting (let profile age a week), adding the address to the website and Google Search Console, and creating citations (e.g., via Fiverr).
- Results vary per attempt; some succeed immediately while others require multiple tries.

### Batch recording, profile creation, and testing 4:30

- Record many short videos (example target: ~15 videos) by swapping printed signs for different cities; estimate ~1.5 hours for 20 videos.
- Create multiple profiles per account (up to three) with slight name variations; delete profiles that fail verification and retry.
- Use the same phone number across profiles or create profiles without a phone number.
- Split-test approaches after verification: leave some profiles to age, run citations on others, and try address submissions on a subset to observe which get approved.
- After addressing unsuccessful attempts, proceed to create live video content as a next step.
