## summary

Eric walks Derek through the batch-verification setup for service-area profiles: shoot one street-sign-and-signage video per city from home, use the home address as the mailing address, pick service areas around it, then throw volume at address approval since single attempts fail more often than they pass.

### How to record verification videos 0:00

- One video works for every profile: street sign, company signage on a car, business card, a dashboard on a device, then unlock and start the car.
- Nothing about the location has to correlate with the mailing address — record from home and swap only the printed sign between cities.
- A decent-looking sign and a paper business card are enough; no special tools needed, just more of the same evidence.

### Mailing address and service area selection 1:46

- The mailing address is wherever the video was recorded, e.g. Derek's home in Flushing.
- Service area is picked around that address: Queens alone for a Queens listing, Queens plus Brooklyn if the address should show in Brooklyn too, and so on — everything between the recorded address and the area you want to rank in.

### Address addition and approval strategy 3:30

- Adding the address is a volume game — most single attempts fail, some people go three-for-three and others fail every time, for no clear reason.
- Odds improve by waiting a week after the profile is listed, and by getting citations plus a Google Search Console entry pointed at the address before adding it — but adding it immediately also works, just at lower odds.
- Recording the same swap-the-sign video ~20 times takes about ninety minutes; five per city (Queens, Brooklyn, Manhattan) gives fifteen attempts to add an address across three profiles per account.

### Batching profiles across gmail accounts 5:46

- Put each city's batch of profiles on its own gmail — all Queens on one, all Brooklyn on another — and delete whichever fail to get approved.
- Slight name variations between profiles; the same phone number can be reused across all of them, or profiles can skip a phone number entirely.
- After verification, split-test: leave some profiles to age untouched, run citations on a couple, and push ten straight away to see what gets through — then move on to a live video for the ones that do not.
