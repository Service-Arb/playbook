## summary

Eric hosts this GMB group call, where members swap tactics for verifying Google Business Profiles
and generating reviews at volume. Devin recaps his history of a service-based suspension and
restart, and the group works through Google's new practice of checking submitted-video locations
for service-area listings, plus a two-per-device rule some members use for switching listings to a
physical address. Members compare live vs. pre-recorded verification, trade signage tricks
(including one member's method of verifying against a random building's exterior with printed
papers, and a pre-recorded-video-feed method for phones rooted onto GrapheneOS), and debate
review-sourcing methods — mall and bus-stop outreach, Home Depot workers, Indeed hires, Facebook
"hiring" posts, and NFC cards — along with how to keep reviews from getting flagged or deleted.
Luca walks through his detailing operation in detail: 26 profiles, roughly $12K in one week,
upselling tactics, a trusted hire who now sets up new profiles for him, and automation plans via
Project Blue and multiple Google Voice numbers for per-profile caller ID. The call closes with
cautionary notes on review pacing — wait several days on a fresh profile — illustrated by one
member's profile that lost most of its reviews after posting them too fast.
