# what kills reviews
- never send the same person to review two of your profiles — Eric
  > one park stand lost 20 reviews in a day; Eric's first guess was repeat reviewers, which the reporter denied
  [2026-09-18](https://www.loom.com/share/c197556c940b4d01b53376dab70d32e5?t=2917)
- (!) review gating: after ~20–30 reviews in a short window google silently stops publishing new ones — `Ilya`
  > not deletion. the reviewer submits and nothing appears
  [2026-09-18](https://www.loom.com/share/c197556c940b4d01b53376dab70d32e5?t=2381)
- google runs periodic sweeps across all GMBs, unrelated to any one profile — `Ilya` lost 10–15% in one wave a month ago
  [2026-09-18](https://www.loom.com/share/c197556c940b4d01b53376dab70d32e5?t=5634)
- so screenshot every review as it lands; the screenshot is what you submit to get it reinstated — `Ilya`, has a VA do it
  [2026-09-18](https://www.loom.com/share/c197556c940b4d01b53376dab70d32e5?t=5655)
- Eric's rule, as restated by `Ilya`: reviews must come from within ~1h proximity of the GMB
  [2026-09-18](https://www.loom.com/share/c197556c940b4d01b53376dab70d32e5?t=4271)
- TODO: after a 1-star lands, subsequent reviews seem to delete at a lower rate — Eric saw it twice, calls it too early to trust
  > suggestion from the floor: try 3-star instead of 1-star, so it can be lived with if it sticks
