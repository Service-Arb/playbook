## info
- no DBA in france; the equivalent is a `nom commercial` attached to the business
- `nom commercial` is the customer-facing identity, must be declared to the RNE, then usable on invoices/quotes/stationery
- micro-entreprise + `nom commercial`: ~30-60 min to file, €0 registration for artisan activity
- normal EI + `nom commercial`: same filing effort, €45 for artisan activity
- SIREN arrives ~2 weeks after the competent authority validates the filing // INPI average
- budget: ~1h of own time + ~2 weeks processing, assuming qualifications/documents already in hand
