# france equivalent of a DBA
- no DBA in france; the equivalent is a `nom commercial` attached to the business
- `nom commercial` is the customer-facing identity, must be declared to the RNE, then usable on invoices/quotes/stationery
- micro-entreprise + `nom commercial`: ~30-60 min to file, €0 registration for artisan activity
- normal EI + `nom commercial`: same filing effort, €45 for artisan activity
- SIREN arrives ~2 weeks after the competent authority validates the filing // INPI average
- budget: ~1h of own time + ~2 weeks processing, assuming qualifications/documents already in hand
  [2026-09-18](https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763)

- plumbing is a regulated artisan activity in france — registration requires the professional qualification or recognised experience
  reason: rules out opening a micro-entreprise purely to manufacture paperwork for maps
  [2026-09-18](https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763)

- stripe atlas: $500 + $100/yr after year 1, delaware entity in ~2 business days — wrong instrument
  reason: delaware registration is not french establishment evidence for a plumber at a lyon address

# one entity, many SIRETs
- each real establishment gets its own SIRET under the one SIREN and appears on the RNE
- a secondary establishment must be a real place where the business operates
- so the per-location unit is an establishment + SIRET, not a separate registration per profile
  [2026-09-18](https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763)

- official business registration, business licence, tax certificates, utility bills (electricity/phone/water/internet)

# naming
- register the plain brand (`Aquafix`), not the keyword string (`Aquafix Plombier Chauffagiste – Lyon, Nord`)
  reason: google requires the GBP name to match real-world usage on site/signage/stationery, and prohibits extra info in the name
- push the keywords into primary + additional category and the service area instead
- same brand within one country → same name on every location, unless real-world branding actually differs
- alternative: if the full string genuinely is the brand, carry it consistently on site, quotes, invoices, vehicles, signage, phone identity and registration
  [2026-09-18](https://chatgpt.com/share/6aacb045-ac9c-83eb-a97d-8624c616a763)
